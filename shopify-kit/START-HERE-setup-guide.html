Sahsih / Shopify

Set up your store in 20 minutes

Upload the theme, import the products, press publish. Everything after that is optional and done by clicking, not coding.

sahsih-shopify-theme.zipThe theme. Upload it as it is. Do not unzip it.
sahsih-products.csvCreates the jelly stick (5 flavours × 2 pack sizes) and The Full Set, with photos.
photos/The same product photos, in case you want to upload them by hand.

01Upload the theme

  1. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store › Themes.
  2. Scroll to Theme library, click Add theme, then Upload zip file.
  3. Choose sahsih-shopify-theme.zip and click Upload file. It takes about a minute.
  4. The theme appears in your library as Sahsih. It is not live yet, so nothing changes for customers.

02Import the products

  1. Go to Products and click Import (top right).
  2. Click Add file, choose sahsih-products.csv, then Upload and preview.
  3. Check the preview shows Sahsih Hangover Jelly Stick and The Full Set, then click Import products.
  4. Shopify emails you when it is done (usually within a minute). The photos come in automatically.

Keep the product handles. The theme finds the products by their handles, sahsih-hangover-jelly-stick and sahsih-full-set, so the Shop section works with no set-up. You can rename the products freely; just leave the handle (the end of the product's web address) as it is, or pick the products in the Shop section settings instead.

Photos missing? Open the product, click Add media and drag in the files from the photos folder. The site still works without them: the 3D stick and built-in box art fill in.

03Check prices and shipping

  • Prices are placeholders ($24 for 1 box, $72 for 3 boxes, $105 for The Full Set). Change them on each product.
  • Weights: set the shipping weight on each variant if your shipping rates depend on weight.
  • Stock: stock is not tracked yet, so everything can be bought. To track it, open a variant, tick Track quantity and enter how many you have. Sold-out options then show as Sold out on the site automatically.

04Preview and publish

  1. Back in Online Store › Themes, find Sahsih and click Customize to see it with your products.
  2. Try it: pick flavours, add to cart, open the cart. Checkout is Shopify's own and uses your payment settings.
  3. When you are happy, click … next to the theme and choose Publish.

05Change anything, no code

Everything lives in Online Store › Themes › Customize. Click a section on the left to edit its words and buttons. Click the gear icon (Theme settings) for everything that applies to the whole site:

Homepage sections, top to bottom: Sahsih hero, Sahsih flavours, Sahsih science, Sahsih when to take, Sahsih shop and Questions. Drag to reorder, click the eye to hide, or Add section for more (Text, Questions, Contact form, Apps).

Add a sixth flavour

  1. On the product, add the new value to the Flavour option (for example Grape) and give it a price for each pack.
  2. In Theme settings › Flavours › Flavour 6, type the same name and pick a colour.
  3. Optional: add a box photo and a stick photo there too. That's it: the dot, the list, the button and the 3D label all appear.

Menus

The header and footer link to the homepage sections by default. To use your own menu, build it in Content › Menus and pick it in the Header or Footer section. A link to /#shop jumps to the Shop section.

06Before you take real orders

  • Payments set up: Settings › Payments
  • Shipping rates: Settings › Shipping and delivery
  • Taxes: Settings › Taxes and duties
  • Refund, privacy and terms policies: Settings › Policies (they appear in the footer automatically)
  • Real prices, weights and stock entered
  • Full ingredient panel and allergen statement written and reviewed. The site says "to be confirmed before launch" until you replace it (Sahsih science section, the Ingredients tab on the product page, the back of the 3D pack, the Questions section).
  • Health wording checked by someone qualified for your market. The copy names ingredients and makes no claims; keep it that way.
  • Password removed when you launch: Online Store › Preferences › Password protection

07If something looks wrong

You seeDo this
"Pick a product for this section" in the Shop sectionImport the products file (step 02), or choose any product in the Shop section's settings.
Upload says the zip is not a valid themeUpload sahsih-shopify-theme.zip itself. If your computer unzipped it automatically, use the original zip from the download.
A flavour button has no colour or does not recolour the siteThe flavour name in Theme settings must match the product's Flavour value exactly (capital letters do not matter).
The 3D stick does not appear on an old phoneNormal. Devices without 3D graphics show the stick photo instead.
The Full Set card is missing from the Shop sectionThe Full Set product must exist (step 02) or be chosen as the Bundle product in the Shop section.
