How it works

Three jobs, one stick.

A big night takes three things off you: your liver works overtime, your fluids and salts go out the window, and your B vitamins go with them. Each Sahsih stick is built around those three.

01

Liver support

Milk thistle

The organ doing the actual cleanup gets the headline ingredient. Milk thistle is the classic botanical for it, and it's in every stick.

02

Hydration

Electrolytes

Water alone doesn't fix a night out. Electrolytes are what your body actually lost, and a jelly delivers them without a pint of liquid you can't face.

03

B vitamins

B1, B6, B12

The three that empty out fastest. Topping them back up is the least glamorous and most useful thing in the formula.
